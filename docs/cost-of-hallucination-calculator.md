## 1. The Financial Impact of Unverified AI Actions

When autonomous agents operate without citation gating, three primary categories of financial loss occur:

```
  Total Monthly Risk = (Pipeline Downtime Cost) + (Triage Labor Cost) + (Regulatory Non-Compliance Fines)
```

1. **Pipeline Downtime Cost**: Unverified dbt modifications or deprecations break downstream analytics, causing executive dashboard downtime.
   - *Cost Rate*: $12,000 / hour of downtime in financial services.
2. **Manual Triage Labor Cost**: Senior platform engineers spending hours manually unravelling unverified agent changes.
   - *Cost Rate*: $150 / hour fully loaded engineering rate × 45 min per incident.
3. **Regulatory Non-Compliance Fines**: Un-audited AI modifications violating GDPR / HIPAA / EU AI Act Article 12 requirements.
